# ILAMA BLOOM design context

ILAMA BLOOM is a bilingual, Egyptian-food-aware nutrition education product. Its public experience should feel editorial, calm, curious and non-judgmental; its private experience should feel trustworthy, factual and easy to recover from.

## Durable decisions

- Palette: warm paper `#f7f0df`, deep green `#142f27`, lime `#dff05f`, coral `#ef715c`, and soft teal `#a7dddb`.
- Typography: expressive serif headlines paired with a plain sans-serif interface layer.
- Voice: practical, conversational Arabic and English. Avoid food morality, shame, streak pressure and treatment promises.
- Public/private boundary: education and games are public; personal logs, questions and visit summaries require an account.
- Child safety: learning-only framing, no public profiles, no leaderboards, no weight targets, and no treatment decisions in game feedback.

## Product signature

The distinctive interaction is “context, not judgment”: familiar Egyptian foods, short explanations, and a factual timeline that helps a person prepare for a visit.
